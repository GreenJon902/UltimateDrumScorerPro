The black line is the actual drawn svg. The red "X" is the anchor / origin (In these visualisations, this will always be (0,0)). The blue lines are the sideSizes (sizeLeft, etc...). The lengths of these lines do not matter, only the perpendicular distance to the anchor. The green lines are the whSizes (width, height). The lengths of these lines are the important detail, the other coordinate is likely just 0. A coloured line being dashed indicates it is a minimum. The light gray background is the background of the svg itself. The darg gray background is the JS calculated bbox of just the symbol / resultOfDrawFunction.
