The black line is the actual drawn svg. The red "X" is the anchor / origin (In these visualisations, this will always be (0,0)). The blue lines are the sideSizes (sizeLeft, etc...). The lengths of these lines do not matter, only the perpendicular distance to the anchor. The green lines appear when there are multiple anchors affecting the side size. Solid is sideSize if the second anchor is minimumAnchorWidth from the first, dashed indicates the actual minimumAnchorWidth. A coloured line being dashed indicates it is a minimum. The light gray background is the background of the svg itself. The darg gray background is the JS calculated bbox of just the symbol / resultOfDrawFunction.

Non-Symbols

We expect the blue lines to be as small as possible, while still entirely containing all pixels drawn by the drawFunc (so they account for the stroke-width).

The blue lines here should be centred on the edge of the stroke.

Symbols

It's harder to describe the disired results here. And I don't yet know if the blue lines account for stroke-width. Look at it and it should look good enough to indicate the side of the symbol - so if a symbol was placed with it's bottom blue line on the top blue line of the symbol below then they don't overlap (too much).

The blue line's inside edge should be the actual line coordinate, and the outside edge match the edge of the stroke.
